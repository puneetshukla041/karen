## RSSB exam calendar and upcoming advertisement plan, Oct 2026 - Mar 2027

### Takeaway
A revised calendar released 28 Sep 2026 lists five exams between 17 Nov 2026 and 5 Feb 2027. It was revised because October exams clashed with municipal and Panchayati Raj elections. All dates are tentative. No future advertisement schedule was found.

### Cited Findings
- Revised calendar of 28 Sep 2026: Teaching Associate 17 Nov and 7-21 Dec 2026 (multiple dates); CET Senior Secondary 1-3 Dec 2026; CET Graduation 16-18 Jan 2027 per one source (another says 16 Dec 2026 to 18 Jan 2027; the application-form page says 1-3 Dec for Graduation, which conflicts); Lab Attendant 2025 on 29 Jan 2027; JE 3-5 Feb 2027. Dates tentative; the stated reason is election-related rescheduling. — [Daily Jagran](https://www.thedailyjagran.com/education/news/rssb-exam-calendar-2026-out-rssb-confirms-dates-for-5-major-exams-including-rajasthan-cet-full-schedule-here-10330882); [FreeJobAlert](https://www.freejobalert.com/articles/rssb-cet-exam-calendar-2026-3070107); [EduAdvice](https://eduadvice.in/educational-news-details/rssb-exam-calender-2026-out-rajasthan-cet-je-other-exam-details/12753)
- The CET is only an eligibility test; department-wise vacancy notices for Patwari, Junior Assistant and others come after results. — [Class24](https://www.class24.study/rajasthan-cet/rssb-cet-vacancy); [FreeJobAlert](https://www.freejobalert.com/articles/rajasthan-rssb-cet-graduation-level-2026-notification-out-apply-online-3056276)
- An aggregator expects about 1,035 Patwari vacancies, which is only an expectation. — [Testbook](https://testbook.com/rajasthan-patwari) (search snippet title only; UNVERIFIED)

### Inferences
- The Patwari, Platoon Commander, Junior Accountant etc. post-specific notices will probably come after the CET Graduation exam and result (roughly Feb-Mar 2027 or later). This is my inference, not a published schedule, and the CET and post notices may or may not need a fresh application.
- Computer-specific posts (Programmer, Informatics Assistant, Junior Technical Assistant) are not on the calendar, so any notice would come outside it.

### Gaps
- The official calendar PDF and the 2026-27 advertisement plan were not retrieved.
- Conflicting CET Graduation exam dates (1-3 Dec 2026 vs 16-18 Jan 2027) are unresolved. The January 2027 date is in the revised-calendar sources, and the December date appears in the aggregator application pages, so the December date is probably stale.

## Eligibility and special qualifications for a B.Tech CS (AI/ML), age 23, General male, Rajasthan domicile

### Takeaway
He is eligible for the CET Graduation Level (any graduate degree) and the graduate posts that follow it, such as Patwari. He is not eligible for JE (civil/mechanical/electrical only) or Teaching Associate (needs a Master's plus NET/PhD). The Computer Instructor posts needed NIELIT/PGDCA or similar and are closed. Everything is UNVERIFIED.

### Cited Findings
- CET Graduation Level: any graduation degree, age 18-40 as on 1 Jan 2027, General fee Rs 600; the window closed 2 Aug 2026, so he is eligible in principle only if he already applied. — [FreeJobAlert](https://www.freejobalert.com/articles/rajasthan-rssb-cet-graduation-level-2026-notification-out-apply-online-3056276)
- Rajasthan Patwari and other CET-linked posts may need additional qualifications (the Rajasthan CCC/RS-CIT-type computer certificate, and for some posts typing), but I found no post-specific notice confirming this. — no source; see Gaps
- JE 874 posts: civil, mechanical and electrical disciplines only, so B.Tech CS does not qualify. — [Search summary, StateJobResult](https://statejobresult.com/rajasthan-rssb-jen-recruitment-2026/)
- Teaching Associate: Master's plus NET/PhD in subject, so B.Tech alone does not qualify. — [Careerpower](https://www.careerpower.in/blog/rssb-teaching-associate-recruitment-2026)
- Computer Instructor: graduation plus NIELIT A Level/PGDCA (Basic) or B Level/MCA (Senior), and an aggregator says age 18-40. Closed 23 Jun 2026. — [ResultBharat](https://www.resultbharat.com/RSSB-Computer-Instructor-2026.html)
- Clerk/Junior Assistant: 12th pass; a B.Tech holder meets the educational minimum, but a computer certificate (Rajasthan requires CCC/O-level or RS-CIT for clerks) was not confirmed in the sources. — [Careers360](https://competition.careers360.com/articles/rssb-ldc-recruitment-2026-released-for-10644-vacancy-apply-online-link)

### Inferences
- B.Tech CS (AI/ML) is a graduate degree, so it meets the "any graduate" CET Graduation and Patwari-type thresholds. For Computer Instructor, B.Tech CS is probably treated as equivalent to a computer qualification by Rajasthan rules, but this needs the notification text.
- Age is no barrier for any listed post.

### Gaps
- Whether the CET Graduation Level applicants can still correct or add anything, and whether the user applied, is unknown.
- Exact post-wise computer qualifications (CCC/O-level, RS-CIT, typing) for Patwari, Junior Assistant and the other CET posts were not found.
- Official links to confirm: rssb.rajasthan.gov.in, sso.rajasthan.gov.in, recruitment.rajasthan.gov.in.
